## Scope and release

- **Alpha proves an end-to-end planting workflow.** The early cohort is approximately 10–15 plants through Brett/Bryan; that is a recruiting aim, not a product cap. The qualitative exit signal is at least three planters active weekly after four weeks, an overseer opening it unprompted, and a positive answer to whether they would recommend it. Early alpha/beta remains free; the intended paying customer is the sending organization, paying per plant. Pricing belongs to beta discovery. The earlier Free = Wiki + Phase 0 / Paid = plant creation model remains an entitlement direction for that work, not an implied paywall in today's code. (2026-07-27, #192/#193.)
- **Finance is deferred; facility management is cut.** F7 finance waits for later scope. F10 facility management is removed from the product direction, not a hidden module awaiting activation. Progress tracking belongs with the Phase Engine instead of a separate F4 feature. (2026-07-26, items 2–4.)
- **Communication scope stays narrow.** SMS and scheduled sends are post-beta. Communication content remains private to the plant; wiki sharing has separate consent. The polymorphic Note entity remains the intended destination even while per-feature notes exist. The communication deferrals and Note direction are not claims about present implementation. (2026-07-26, items 5, 7, 8.)
- **Wiki serves usable guidance.** Keep downloads and browsing results. Video is cut from near-term scope; producing our own material is a later possibility. Network/sending-church customization requires further discovery. Generated-document templates are code-defined; they are not a new database-backed authoring system. (2026-07-26/27, items 6, 15, 14.)
- **Deletion and transfer remain separate future work.** Church deletion stays out of alpha and goes through support. Ownership transfer needs its own audit and notification design; removing an account's access is not ownership transfer or deletion of the underlying person. (#187, #342, #185.)
- **Evry is retired.** The chat experience and its implementation direction in #758 were superseded by #854. Preserve historical tables/data and versioned migrations. Langfuse remains for Planting Intelligence; retiring one consumer does not retire that integration. (2026-09-26, #854.)

## Authority and invitations

- **Keep the seat model flat.** Owner, Admin and Member apply within a plant, sending church or network. Ownership controls relationship-level choices; Admin covers ordinary administration. Org Members receive the same oversight read scope as other org seats, not the plant's individual CRM. Do not grow a per-module permissions grid merely because the current capability map could express one. (2026-08-20, #185; detailed authority in the account-seats requirements.)
- **Coaching is an assignment, not tenancy.** It gives read access to the named plant's own records, independently of that plant's oversight sharing toggles, because the plant explicitly chose the coach. It grants no writes and no person record. A coach may separately hold a seat in a tenancy; those two scopes do not lend authority to each other. (#185, #496, #568.)
- **Seat invitations refuse tenancy moves, not existing accounts as a category.** An account with neither a seat nor a tenancy can accept its first seat while signed in, retaining coaching assignments. An associated discovery account must first leave its association. This supersedes the old register-only invitation direction. (Sebastian, 2026-09-09, #568; discovery #294.)
- **Discovery is a deliberate identity.** It is not a fallback interpretation of every account without a tenancy. Its oversight association grants no org seat or tenant access; converting to a plant or accepting a seat must respect the existing relationship. (#294, carried from invariants.)
- **An invitation must be answerable without revealing account existence.** Sender-facing results must not expose whether an address has an account, including through payloads or pending-list details. A valid open emailed invitation may describe its inviter to the recipient so the consent is meaningful. Association acceptance never silently replaces an existing association. (#254 and invitation rulings carried from invariants.)
- **Resend is an email action, not a delivery ledger.** Do not persist an `email_sent` status: provider acceptance is not delivery. The resulting limits on cooldown claims are deliberate; see [accepted limitations](#accepted-limitations). (Invitation resend ruling, carried from invariants.)
- **Either side can end an oversight association.** Require deliberate confirmation, notify the other side, and keep the relationship audit. Authority follows the subject and the side ending it; granting a seat is a different action. (#274, OV-007/OV-010.)
- **Removing a seat removes login authority, not CRM history.** Preserve the person's record and ordinary team membership; release access-dependent leadership/task ownership so the plant can reassign work. Explicit team-leader appointments retain their provenance and outrank role-derived leadership. Historical ambiguous rows remain legacy facts rather than invented attributions. (#185, #830.)

## Privacy and oversight

- **Invite-origin sharing is a disclosed default, not covert consent.** A plant's first invitation-origin association starts sharing enabled, after the invitation explains it. A self-started plant begins with sharing disabled. The rationale is the intended org-paid model: a sponsoring organization paying for a plant with no visibility is a failed workflow. Later associations must preserve choices already made. (#187/#193, 2026-08-15; #620.)
- **Relationship visibility differs from private feature data.** The org portfolio and aggregate relationship context remain visible without implying permission to pull private plant records. Sharing gates control feature detail. A network's sending-church list is scoped to that network, not every organization attached to its plants. (#186/#187; oversight rulings carried from invariants.)
- **Turning sharing off announces only the change.** Tell overseeing orgs coarsely, without naming the toggle or creating a stream that reconstructs it. This notice is consent-exempt so a toggle cannot suppress its own announcement. Turning sharing on does not need that warning. (#187, CS-012.)
- **Oversight pull and notification preferences are different consent.** A plant controls what may be disclosed; an org controls whether and when it receives permitted push/digest updates. Org preferences never widen plant consent. (#224/#225, #448.)
- **Disclosure changes with infrastructure.** The beta sharing change and its disclosure must ship together. Vendor/model retention claims belong in [data posture](features/phase-engine/data-posture.md), with current verification; historical pricing or retention estimates are not standing promises. (NFR-PE4, 2026-07-26 item 16.)
- **Personal context stays private by default.** Oversight address views expose locality, not the street/door. Photos use private access, not public raw object URLs. Feedback may bridge to a public GitHub issue, but system-attached identity details must not: the user is told their submitted description will be published. The internal feedback record owns its lifecycle; GitHub is a one-way convenience link. (#618, #617/#654, #190, carried from invariants.)

## Planting methodology and intelligence

- **The Playbook judges the journey; software does not advance it automatically.** Phase is a soft constraint. Initial declaration is the planter's attestation, different from a later transition. Journey onboarding asks whether the planter has attested, not whether a default phase column is populated. (#271, #675, carried from invariants.)
- **The biweekly vision-meeting target is ruled.** Keep the Playbook target of at least one meeting every two weeks; practitioner weekly cadence is appropriate after the core team forms. This supersedes C12's earlier deferral. Do not tell weekly planters they are over-meeting. (#538 / #483 C12, 2026-08-21.)
- **Unknown is not healthy.** An unmeasured and unattested lens cannot produce a positive assessment. Neutral guidance remains useful at cold start: the planter needs the missing evidence named and a first step, not an empty panel. (#483 C17, #635.)
- **Guidance and commitments remain distinct.** Insights advise; tasks commit. Keep phase-triggered task templates. Follow-up belongs to first-time vision-meeting attendees, due meeting day plus two, rather than manufacturing the same follow-up for returning/core attendees. (#323, #96; 2026-07-26 item 9.)
- **Checklists support judgment.** Completing subtasks does not decide completion of the parent task. Checklists are not independent tasks for counts and reporting; recurrence starts a fresh checklist. Leadership training belongs to the team rather than separate role assignments because planting teams are small. (#90, #370; 2026-07-27 item 12b.)
- **Team Playbook responsibilities are offered once.** Deleting an offered item is a user's choice; later visits must not resurrect it. Template identity survives a renamed team. (#311, #409-2, carried from invariants.)
- **Answers about the plant belong to the plant.** Attestation prompts do not ask each user to answer the same question independently. User-facing facts with the same meaning should be phrased consistently; raw storage paths are not explanation. (#411, #394, carried from invariants.)
- **Planting Intelligence is a structured assessment pipeline.** The chosen direction is a plain TypeScript/Vercel AI SDK pipeline rather than an agent framework for the judge. Keep rubric history interpretable across versions, and keep self-hosted Langfuse for evaluation/observability. Source owns the active model, scheduler, embedding shape and version constants. (Phase Engine technical rulings; #538; Evry exception superseded by #854.)
- **Launch Sunday is an outcome, not another meeting type.** Do not infer a phase promotion from recording it. Post-launch service tracking needs separate discovery. (#271.)

## Experience and scheduling

- **Church time is intentional.** Default new plants to America/Chicago; do not infer it from an address or add a timezone step to onboarding. The owner can change it. Notification schedules use the church's local day/time. Sunday 16:00 was chosen after the gathering, not as a UTC boundary; subsequent church preferences govern delivery. (#166, #135, #448.)
- **Settings preserve the current workspace.** The hash-driven modal supersedes the old ban on hash settings and the earlier two-tab route plan. It should open over the current page with a real dialog's focus behavior. Block section changes and dismissal until drafts are saved or reverted; keep account-specific state separate. (#657, #647/#673; implementation/cache experiments remain in source.)
- **Church creation makes onboarding step 1 non-reenterable.** Replace that step in history; Back from step 2 deliberately leaves the flow. A read-only completed step was rejected as needless UI for backward navigation. The finish screen has no reopenable step URL or contextual guide. (#390.)
- **Planting Intelligence uses a stable reading surface.** Keep sectioned assessments and sticky check-in controls, preserve drafts, and use the page heading as the sole h1. This is the selected direction, not a new comparison of prototype variants. (#533, carried from invariants.)
- **Accessibility and identity both matter.** WCAG AA contrast is binding; APCA is advisory. Inline prose links remain underlined. The chosen status-badge direction is Tinted Editorial: prospects stay neutral, attendee/launch-team statuses share a hue. The authenticated app restores shadcn geometry. Marketing remains Sharp. Brand color remains coherent across both. (#386, #387, #429, #688; exact design values in `DESIGN.md`.)

## Accepted limitations

These entries preserve explicit acceptance of a gap, which observing the implementation alone does not establish. They are not permission to spread the gap to another flow. Remove an entry when the owning fix closes it.

- **Registration versus invitation revocation:** registration inserts the account before its batched invitation claim; a revoke between description and that batch can leave a seat on the account with the invitation revoked. This was accepted in the original registration path; signed-in acceptance has its own guarded path. Closing this gap requires making the registration grant conditional on a valid claim. (`src/app/(auth)/register/actions.ts`; former multi-tenancy invariant.)
- **Coach-assignment author:** any plant Admin can end an assignment, although AS-018 describes the creating Admin. Assignment rows do not retain that author. The accepted scope remains until provenance is carried onto the assignment. (`src/lib/auth/seat-rules.ts`; former seats invariant.)
- **Household creation:** a person soft-deleted between the two READ COMMITTED batch statements can leave an inert orphan household. A later JavaScript error cannot roll back the committed batch. The narrow window and inert result were deliberately accepted; atomic transport alone does not prove the stronger claim in the source comment. (`src/lib/people/household.ts`; former transaction invariant.)
- **Resend cooldown:** the org invitation cooldown display is client-session-local, so reloads/another admin can misstate it; provider deduplication still constrains actual delivery. Seat resends rotate credentials and therefore use separate provider keys: concurrent admins may send two messages, with the later link superseding the earlier one. This follows the no-persisted-send-status decision, not a promise of a global cooldown. (Former multi-tenancy invariant.)

- **Consent-exempt notification history:** an exempt milestone remains visible to its original recipient after the plant leaves the portfolio or that recipient is re-seated. This was accepted as that account's own relationship history, not disclosure to another account. Closing the gap requires stamping and backfilling organization anchors on exempt milestones. (#527, from #526's review; former multi-tenancy invariant.)
- **Sending-church audit history:** events about a sending church are recorded but the existing history reader filters by plant. The write-only record was accepted until a subject-aware history view is built. (`src/lib/invitations/history.ts`; former multi-tenancy invariant.)
