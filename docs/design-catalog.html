01 · Palette

Three colors. Two dark pairings, not three.

Cream warmed to a true cream #FBF8EA — red leads green, so it no longer reads pale-green. 04 · Voice

Warm, direct, honest about the work.

Grounded shepherd, ruled 2026-07-27. Short declaratives, concrete nouns, "you" always. We promise clarity, never growth — growth isn't ours to promise.

The pitch

Your church plant, understood.

EveryField puts a proven planting methodology to work on your real progress — the people, meetings, and momentum that get you to launch Sunday — and tells you what deserves your attention this week.

How we speak

  • Short declaratives. One idea per sentence. "You stay the shepherd; it keeps watch."
  • Concrete nouns. People, meetings, giving, launch Sunday — never "workflows" or "solutions".
  • Honest about difficulty. "Planting a church is one of the hardest things you'll ever do."
  • "You", always. The planter is the subject; the software is the servant.
  • Sentence case; verb-first buttons. "Request an invite."
  • Promise clarity, not growth. Outcomes belong to God; visibility belongs to us.

Never

  • No AI hype — "AI-powered", "intelligent insights", "supercharge" are banned.
  • No churchy insider-speak on the marketing surface — no "Kingdom impact", no "unleash".
  • No exclamation marks. No "oops". No emoji.
  • No fake urgency, countdowns, or dark patterns.
  • No stock photography. The product, the mark, or commissioned art.
  • No "users". Planters, coaches, sending churches, networks.

05 · Components

Rectangles. Ink leads, green signals.

Cards are flat surfaces with defined 1px rings at radius zero — no washes, no shadows-as-decoration. Buttons are rectangles: ink fills, 1px-outline ghosts, hovers that invert. Green is spent as a signal — registration squares, active rules — and once as the closing panel.

09 · Discipline

The system holds because of what's left out.

Including what was tried and retired — from the 2026-07-29 feedback pass and the 2026-07-30 side-by-side ruling that settled the direction.

Do

  • Grounds stay cream (#FBF8EA). Color arrives through art, registration squares, and one ink panel.
  • Dark pairings are cream-on-ink and ink-on-green — nothing else.
  • Field green #0B7A3F carries green's meaning in text and links.
  • Rectangles everywhere: radius zero, defined 1px rings, corner marks on art panels. Edges are drawn, not implied.
  • CTAs are ink — fills or 1px outlines, hovers invert. Green fills exactly one closing panel per page, its button ink.
  • Display type is light and giant: Outfit 400 at −3%, line-height 1 — scale talks, weight doesn't. Newsreader is the marketing body voice; never in UI.
  • Long content earns an interactive toggle — feature switcher, phase tabs — before it earns more scroll.

Retired by ruling

  • The "radiant, refined" world entire — pills, rounded corners, ground-line marker, green primary CTA, weight-600 display — retired 2026-07-30 after a side-by-side: "too soft… looks uncared for." Sharp is the direction.
  • Green text or accents on ink — 10:1 legible, but too much. Gone everywhere.
  • Green text on cream — 1.7:1, illegible. Fill or nothing.
  • Green-wash panels behind cards — replaced by card surfaces with drawn edges.
  • Radial glows and soft gradients — "AI-looking". Flat panels only.
  • Marker pills, then the ground-line marker — both replaced by the registration square.
  • Gray-bar skeletons as product stand-ins — lazy. Shots are full product frames with real words, on the field paintings, until real screenshots replace them.
  • The boxed switcher list with a 3px edge rule (v3) — selection read louder than the product. Now a thin rule + a breath of tint.
  • Decorative cropped marks and the generative field pattern — parked for a dedicated exploration, not in the system today.
  • No stock photos, no exclamation marks, no "AI-powered" — as always.
