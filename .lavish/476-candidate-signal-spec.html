3 · Rubric v1 Lens 7 delta — individual pipeline (into the D0 draft)

  • The 60-day behavioral pattern opens a conversation; it never concludes one. Insight type is "worth a leadership conversation."
  • Interview / 4 C's records deepen the prompt: present → cite the recorded human judgment and its date; absent → "no interview recorded yet — the 5-criteria interview is the next step", stated as a next step, never as a mark against the person.
  • Character, doctrine, gifting, relational maturity, teachability are named as human territory — the judge may not infer them from any fact.
  • Individual candidate signals are planter-audience only (existing aggregate-only network rule restated here).
  • Coverage-gap copy ("no roles assigned") is #481, not this issue.

4 · Acceptance criteria (draft)

  1. AC-1 Rename. LeadershipReadinessSignal → LeadershipCandidateSignal across signals, snapshot, trends, and tests; snapshot compatibility preserved (old persisted snapshots still read).
  2. AC-2 Threshold fact. candidateThresholdDays: 60 joins the snapshot; the judge cites it, never invents it.
  3. AC-3 Interview feed. Per-candidate interview / 4 C's facts per D1; SQL in signals/queries.ts; tests cover a candidate with and without records.
  4. AC-4 Rubric. The D0 draft carries the Lens 7 individual-pipeline delta (§3); the banned phrases are absent from v1; Bryan's conversation copy is the register.
  5. AC-5 Works. Snapshot test proves the renamed signal + new facts reach the judge prompt; no UI change, no browser gate.

5 · Boundaries

  • No schema change — interviews and assessments exist.
  • No auto-assigning ministry roles from the signal.
  • Coverage-gap wording — #481. The 60-day number itself was confirmed by C22 ("keep the signal, change the name").
