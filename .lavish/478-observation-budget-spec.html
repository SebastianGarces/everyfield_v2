2 · The design

2.1 · Rubric: the budget is the judge's discipline

  • Rubric v1 §6: for the planter audience, the assessment names exactly one primary focus and at most two supplements — three planter insights, hard cap, primary listed first.
  • Enforced structurally, not just by prose: the judge output schema caps planter-audience insights at 3 (zod refinement); the model retries on violation. Old persisted assessments (4–7 insights) remain valid to read.
  • Everything else the judge would have said lives where it already lives: the CSF scorecard, exit criteria, and fact drill-downs. The budget forces prioritization, not silence.
  • Network-audience insights are untouched here (#482's turf).
