3 · Rubric v1 Lens 5 delta (into the D0 draft)

  • "What feeds it" becomes the two rhythm attestations and their freshness; the Prayer Leader title is removed from Lens 5 and cited only as Lens 7 coverage.
  • Healthy: rhythm established and reaffirmed within the window; prayer woven into gatherings.
  • An unanswered prayer attestation is unknown, never healthy — the judge says "we do not have enough information to assess prayer health" (the full evidence-quality vocabulary is #483; Lens 5 states the local rule now).
  • A stale attestation is cited honestly: "you confirmed a prayer rhythm, 45 days ago — is it still happening?"
