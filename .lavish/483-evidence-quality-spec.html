1 · The design

1.1 · A deterministic evidence profile per lens

  • The snapshot gains evidence.byLens: for each of the 8 lenses, the strongest evidence available — measured (SQL-derived facts exist), attested (only manual signals speak, and they are answered), or unknown (nothing measured, nothing answered). Computed from a lens → fact-paths map, pure SQL/code, tested.
  • inferred is deliberately NOT a snapshot value — a fact is never inferred; only a conclusion can be. The rubric governs inference (below); the profile stays honest.
  • Stale attestations (the #474/#475 reaffirm windows) degrade the profile's phrasing ("attested, 45 days ago") but not its category — freshness already lives in the facts.

1.2 · Surfaces — derived, never synced

  • CSF scorecard: a lens whose profile is unknown renders "Insufficient evidence — we don't have enough information to assess prayer health" (Bryan's phrasing), visually distinct from healthy AND from needs-attention. Never a quiet blank.
  • Insight cards: an evidence badge (measured / attested) derived from the insight's category → lens profile. No new persisted field — one source of truth.
  • Network: unknown lenses phrase as insufficient evidence; at the portfolio level this composes with #480's Limited-visibility posture.

1.3 · Rubric v1 — the evidence rules (into the D0 draft)

  • Every conclusion states its basis. The snapshot's per-lens profile is handed to the judge as facts; the judge may not claim stronger evidence than the profile grants.
  • Unknown ≠ healthy. A lens with an unknown profile produces at most an "insufficient evidence" statement — never a pass, never silence read as health.
  • Inferred conclusions are named and constrained: the judge may infer only where the rubric explicitly permits it, and the copy must name the inference and its weakness (the Lens 2 pattern from #470 is the template).
  • The Appendix C Q2 placeholder (prayer/generosity nearly invisible) resolves: thin coverage is shown as thin, and #474/#475's attestations are what upgrade it.

D1 · Confirm derive-don't-persist confirm

The alternative is a persisted evidence_quality column on insights (judge-claimed, migration, second source of truth that can drift from the facts). The lean shape derives every badge from the deterministic profile.

2 · Acceptance criteria (draft)

  1. AC-1 Profile. evidence.byLens in the snapshot, computed from a single lens → fact-paths map; unit tests cover measured, attested-only, and unknown lenses (prayer unanswered is the canonical case).
  2. AC-2 Scorecard. Unknown lenses render the insufficient-evidence state with Bryan's phrasing; visually distinct from healthy and needs-attention; cursor-pointer where clickable.
  3. AC-3 Badges. Insight cards show the derived evidence badge; no new persisted field (per D1).
  4. AC-4 Rubric. The D0 draft carries the evidence-quality section (§1.3): basis stated, unknown ≠ healthy, inference named and constrained, Appendix C Q2 resolved.
  5. AC-5 Works. Browser validation: a plant with unanswered prayer toggles shows "Insufficient evidence" on the scorecard; a snapshot test proves the profile reaches the judge prompt.
