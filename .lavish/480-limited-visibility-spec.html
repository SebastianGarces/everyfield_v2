1 · Today, verified

classifyPlantHealth (phase-engine/oversight/read.ts) is pure and deliberately fed only the network insights that survived privacy gating. Postures: on-track · watch · readiness. A fully-private plant has zero visible insights and no escalating launch state → on-track. The classifier cannot tell "nothing wrong" from "nothing visible" — the caller never tells it anything was withheld.

I would not want absence of warning signs to accidentally look like an "on track" signal. — Bryan, C11

2 · The design

2.1 · A fourth posture, with a precedence rule

  • PlantHealthClassification gains limited-visibility. The classifier learns what was withheld: callers pass the gating outcome (shared features / count of insights hidden by gating) alongside the visible insights.
  • Precedence: real signals still win. readiness and watch fire from visible insights exactly as today — a visible critical is a readiness conversation regardless of what else is hidden. What limited-visibility replaces is the on-track claim: when anything was withheld by gating, or no assessment-bearing category is shared at all, the plant can never read "on track."
  • Order: readiness → watch → limited-visibility → on-track.

2.2 · The surfaces

  • Portfolio views (plants directory, plant-health portfolio): a neutral "Limited visibility" badge — visually distinct from the three health postures, not a warning color; silence is a fact, not a fault.
  • Plant detail: the posture line reads Bryan's wording — "Plant has chosen not to share assessment data." — beside the existing explain-why empty states (OV-002 already explains per-section gating; this is the portfolio-level companion).
  • Oversight digest/summary surfaces reuse the same posture value — one vocabulary (`health-presentation.ts`).

2.3 · Rubric v1 §7 delta (into the D0 draft)

The posture vocabulary becomes four-valued, and the rule is stated: a plant that shares no assessment data (or whose gating withholds observations) is presented as Limited visibility — never On track, Worth a look, or Readiness focus on the strength of silence. The v0 "please confirm" on the privacy trade is answered in the text: the trade stands (privacy wins), and this posture is its honest label. Escalations from visible data still apply.
