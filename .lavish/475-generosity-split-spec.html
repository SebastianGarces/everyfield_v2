2 · The design

D1 · One lens or two? pick one

The 8 CSFs are the Playbook's list, and generosity is a persisted category slug (scorecard, judge enum — same shape as the #473 rename). Splitting into a ninth category breaks the "8 CSF" frame everywhere it appears; keeping one lens with two separately-scored signals keeps the frame and still forbids the collapse.
