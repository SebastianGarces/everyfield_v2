2 · Decisions

D1 · The name pick one

Bryan offered both. "Engagement" collides with two things we already say: "breadth of engagement" is one of this very lens's signals, and "engagement" is scattered through marketing copy — the same word naming a lens and a signal inside it would blur. "Cohesion" names the group property the attendance signals actually approximate, and it is the word Bryan led with.

Queued ✓

D2 · How deep pick one

The slug is part of the persisted vocabulary. With zero existing rows and no production database yet, a full rename costs one enum edit plus a defensive UPDATE migration — and the codebase never carries two words for one lens.

Queued ✓
