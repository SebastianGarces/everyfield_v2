1 · The settled design

A follow-up "has a clear owner" when an open task (category='follow_up', relatedType='person') exists for that contact with assignedToId set to a live, still-committed member. Owners are accounts — coherent with the seat chain (#495 merged, #497/#500 queued). Every seat-holder already has a person row with status leader (account-person.ts, verified), so the D2 guard passes for seat-holders until a demotion.

1.1 · New deterministic facts (FollowUpSignals)

Fact Definition Phrase
unownedCount Open follow-up contacts with no open, assigned follow-up task — covers "task unassigned" and "no task at all." "4 follow-ups have no clear owner"
staleUnownedCount Stale ∩ unowned. "3 stale follow-ups have no owner"
distinctOwnerCount Distinct assignedToId across open follow-up tasks. "follow-up is spread across 5 people"
planterOwnedCount Open follow-up tasks assigned to the Owner-seat account. "you own 6 of the 9 open follow-ups"
