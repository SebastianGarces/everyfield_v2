2 · The design

2.1 · Rubric v1 Lens 3 preface (into the D0 draft)

  • Lens 3 opens with the framing: "50 minimum / 100 target are this planting methodology's benchmarks (Playbook-derived). Different contexts and models reasonably launch at very different sizes — these numbers are not a universal definition of a healthy plant."
  • The judge reports distance and trajectory relative to the benchmark. It may not call a plant unhealthy, or its size a failure, for being under 50.
  • Insight copy says "the Playbook benchmark", never "the requirement" or "needed for a healthy launch".

2.2 · Wiki sweep — with a lever, because the corpus is a database

Wiki content lives only in wiki_articles (a protected table — no repo seed), so the sweep cannot be a normal diff. The work ships a committed, re-runnable audit script:

  • scripts/audit-benchmark-language.ts — read-only: flags global articles where 50/100 appears with gate phrasing ("must", "required", "minimum needed", "cannot launch") and prints the offending sentences.
  • Content edits reframe each of the 10 articles: the number stays, prefixed as the methodology's benchmark; gate verbs become benchmark verbs. Edits are applied to the canonical corpus via a companion script run once, and the audit re-run proves zero findings remain.
  • The audit stays in the repo — it is the regression guard when future wiki content lands.
